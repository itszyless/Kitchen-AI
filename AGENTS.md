## Documentation and communication

Update the main README when a significant feature, setup requirement, architecture decision, or limitation changes. Do not update it for every minor edit. Honor explicit requests to leave it unchanged.

Use clear, professional language suitable for developers and general readers. Describe concrete implementation and validation accurately, acknowledge AI-assisted development, and do not overstate the owner's expertise or production readiness. Avoid long dashes and unnecessary beginner-oriented commentary.

## UI and monetization research

Before onboarding, visual-system or subscription changes, consult `docs/UI-SUBSCRIPTION-RESEARCH.md`. Preserve the owner's light-default SF Pro direction. Distinguish research-backed constraints from proposed experiments; demonstrate actual cooking value, use truthful subscription terms and avoid unsupported conversion or outcome claims. Research proposals are not implemented features.
